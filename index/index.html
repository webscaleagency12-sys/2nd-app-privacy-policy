1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Quote Spark ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android mobile application (the "App", Package Name: com.aistudio.dailyspark.krvmqa).

By downloading, installing, or using Daily Quote Spark, you consent to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Daily Quote Spark operates primarily as an on-device utility. The following data is processed and stored exclusively on your device using secure local SQLite storage (Room Database) and Jetpack DataStore:

  • User Preferences: Your preferred language selection, chosen quote categories (e.g., Motivation, Wisdom, Faith, Poetry), and display mode preferences.
  • Favorites: Quotes and wisdom entries that you explicitly mark as a favorite.
  • Notification Preferences: Daily notification frequency (1 to 3 times per day) and user-configured delivery times.
  • Shown History (No-Repeat Engine): A local identifier record of previously displayed quotes used strictly on-device to prevent repetitive quotes until a full category cycle completes.

B. Information Transmitted Over the Network

  • On-Demand Translation: If you explicitly choose to translate an individual quote into another language, the text of that specific quote and the requested destination language code are securely sent to Google Gemini API over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No user account credentials, persistent identifiers, or device-identifying information are linked or transmitted with this request.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No user registration, passwords, or personal account profiles are required or collected.
  • No location data (GPS or network-based coordinates).
  • No biometric, camera, microphone, or sensor data.
  • No contacts, address book, SMS, or external storage files.
  • No financial, banking, or payment card information.

3. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

Our application requests only the minimum necessary permissions required to provide its core functionality:

Permission Classification Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Permission (Android 13+) Required strictly to deliver your scheduled daily quote and wisdom notifications at the specific time(s) you select.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal / System Feature Allows the Android AlarmManager and WorkManager to trigger daily notification reminders reliably, even after a device reboot.
INTERNET Normal Permission Required solely to process on-demand quote translation requests via the cloud language translation API.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

The App relies on trusted, standard industry service providers that adhere to rigorous privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core platform services, app distribution, and system framework support. Google's privacy policy can be viewed at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google Gemini API / Cloud Services: Used strictly for stateless language translation when user-triggered. Requests do not contain personal identifiers and comply with Google's API service terms.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Daily Quote Spark does not display advertisements and does not integrate with any third-party ad networks, marketing brokers, or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other applications or websites.

6. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

Because all personal preferences, favorite quotes, and history are kept locally on your phone:

  • Full User Control: You can remove individual quotes from your favorites list at any time with a single tap.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can instantly delete all local data by going to Android Settings > Apps > Daily Quote Spark > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • No residual data remains on any external servers because we do not maintain central user databases.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Daily Quote Spark is suitable for general audiences and offers wholesome motivational quotes and literature. However, the App is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

8. Security Measures

We are committed to protecting your data:

  • All network transmissions (such as quote translation) are conducted over modern, secure Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption.
  • Local storage uses standard Android sandboxed storage, preventing other installed apps from accessing your preferences or favorites.
